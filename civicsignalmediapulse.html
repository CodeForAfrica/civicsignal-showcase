What this is

A working showcase built from a scraped corpus of African digital-native publishers — titles, body text, country and publication date, run through a lightweight topic & language classifier.

How topics are assigned

Each article is scored against bilingual (English/French) keyword sets across eight topics; the highest-scoring topic wins. Articles matching nothing are left as "Other" and excluded from the charts above.

Known limits

Publication dates lean heavily on recent scrape runs, keyword classification is heuristic rather than semantic, and Arabic-language coverage is detected but not yet topic-classified with dedicated keywords.
