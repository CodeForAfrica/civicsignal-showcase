How CivicSignal makes sense of the creator economy

Mapping framework, 2026

CivicSignal's mapping framework is grounded in a distinction among three related but conceptually separate functions:

  • Content creation is a production activity: the development and presentation of content for an audience.
  • Influence is an audience relationship and effect: the capacity to engage an audience and shape its attention, interpretation, attitudes, or behaviour.
  • Journalism is a professional function and method: the production of public-interest information through reporting, verification, editorial judgement, and adherence to professional and ethical standards.

These functions frequently overlap — an actor may be a content creator, an influencer, a journalist, or a combination of the three.

Content creator Creator–influencer Influencer Creator–journalist Journalist–influencer Journalist

Three layers, not one score

  • Actor identity & role — who they are, and what functions they perform.
  • Practices & capacities — how they produce, distribute, finance and govern the work.
  • Ecosystem position & outcomes — their reach, trust and influence, and the risks and resourcing gaps they face.

Why it matters

The goal isn't a follower-count leaderboard. It's a fuller picture of who's producing and interpreting information, what standards and resources back their work, and where targeted support — funding, training, protection — could strengthen Africa's independent media ecosystem.
