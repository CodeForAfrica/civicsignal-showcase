A mature creator economy, unevenly paid.

A survey of 42 South African creators and independent journalists — who they are, what they do, how they sustain the work, and where it ties back into institutional journalism. A partnership between Code for Africa, the Center for News, Technology and Innovation (CNTI), and ProjectC.

Reach doesn't cleanly predict monetization. Some sub-25k creators report full monetization; some with 500k–1M+ report only partial.

Journalists who identify unambiguously as "Yes" monetize fully at a higher rate (12/27, 44%) than those who say "it's complicated" (3/11, 27%).
