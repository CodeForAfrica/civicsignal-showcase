Why this matters now

Africa's information ecosystem is moving faster than any one-time map can capture.

This is what the world's largest annual study of news consumption found this year. Code for Africa is a named sponsor of the Reuters Institute's Digital News Report 2026.

DNR 2026 · Global
Social & video networks54%
News orgs' own sites & apps51%

For the first time, social media and video networks are used weekly for news by more people globally than news organisations' own websites and apps.

DNR 2026 · Global
Get news from creators46%
Trust in news overall37%

Get some news from creators of any type — not a fringe behaviour, a mainstream one. Trust in news overall has fallen to its lowest recorded level.

DNR 2026 · Kenya
WhatsApp (+16pp)62%
TikTok (+20pp)58%

WhatsApp reach for news in Kenya, with TikTok close behind — both jumped in a single year. Neither is visible to a website crawler.

A second, newer footprint

Legacy and online media is our foundation layer. We are adding an emerging media layer.

MediaData and MediaCloud, above, map and monitor Africa's established news industry — licensed outlets, broadcasters, news agencies. But a growing share of African news and commentary now happens outside that industry entirely: independent digital-native publishers, individual creator-journalists, and newsletters, Telegram channels and fediverse accounts that never touch a crawlable website. Three source types, unified into one live view.

Reports & publications

Research built on CivicSignal's infrastructure.

The same monitoring capability behind Wavelength also powers investigative research — identifying outlets, tracking their publication patterns over time, and cross-referencing coverage against known actors and narratives.

CivicSignal / CfA staff-authored External research

Media Capture: How foreign states infiltrate media in francophone West Africa

CivicSignal · GIZ SRIEWA partnership (2026 report)

Traces how state-linked entities in Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal and Niger drew outlets and journalists into relationships that embedded foreign narratives and distorted editorial practice.

Healthcare R&D as reflected in digital news: Kenya, Nigeria & South Africa

Emily Boardman Ndulue, Sandra Roberts & Praise Ocheanya Abraham · AJIC, No. 34 (2024)

Filtered 5M+ articles down to 2,917 relevant pieces using CivicSignal's MediaCloud infrastructure to examine how healthcare R&D is covered in African digital news.

MediaMeltdown

CivicSignal Research · #MediaMeltdown series (2025)

A four-part series tracking how funding shocks — the pandemic, then US aid cuts — have reshaped Africa's community media, and how newsrooms are adapting.

Mapping Responses to Social Media Threats

Lisa Schirch · Toda Peace Institute, Policy Brief No. 38 (2019)

Surveys the threats social media poses to peace and democracy — surveillance, addiction, disinformation, hate speech, polarisation — and maps civic-tech and policy responses to each.

AI Hype Through an African Lens

Sisanda Nkoala, Trust Matsilele, Musawenkosi Ndlovu & Tanja Bosch · Digital Journalism (2025)

A critical analysis of how African online news publications use language to frame AI, treating that framing itself as a form of symbolic action.

“Wake up and Think of the Children!”

M. Zitmane & E. Lāma · in The New Communication Revolution (2023)

Examines the ambivalent relationship between motherhood, femininity and anti-vaccination sentiment in online discourse.

AI Solutions for Election Integrity

Nicholas Sesugh Iwokwagh & Paul Uko Obagwu · in AI and the Future of Democracy (2025)

Assesses how AI-based tools can help detect and counter election-related disinformation and safeguard democratic processes.

Combining Content Analysis With In-Depth Interviews

Raheemat Adeniran · SAGE Research Methods Cases (2026)

A methods case study on mixing content analysis with editor interviews to examine Nigerian newspaper coverage of maternal and child health.
