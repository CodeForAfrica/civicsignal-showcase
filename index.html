Why this matters now

Africa's information ecosystem is moving faster than any one-time map can capture.

This isn't a CivicSignal talking point — it's what the world's largest annual study of news consumption found this year. Code for Africa is a named sponsor of the Reuters Institute's Digital News Report 2026, and CfA's Chris Roper co-authored its South Africa chapter.

DNR 2026 · Global
54% vs 51%

For the first time, social media and video networks are used weekly for news by more people globally than news organisations' own websites and apps.

DNR 2026 · Global
46%

Get some news from creators of any type — not a fringe behaviour, a mainstream one. Trust in news overall has fallen to its lowest recorded level (37%).

DNR 2026 · Kenya
62% (+16pp)

WhatsApp reach for news in Kenya, with TikTok close behind at 58% (+20pp) — both jumped in a single year. Neither is visible to a website crawler.
