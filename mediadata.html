CivicSignal · MediaData Registry · Ownership · Reach

The registry behind every outlet on the map.

MediaData is the underlying registry of Africa's licensed media houses — who owns what, what they reach, and how that changes over time. It's the foundation layer everything else on CivicSignal is mapped against.

What it is

A registry, not a crawl.

Where MediaCloud collects what outlets publish, MediaData records what an outlet is: its ownership structure, licensing status, and reach — mapped once by CfA's research teams and kept current rather than inferred from scraped content.

Coverage

Licensed & established media

Broadcasters, newspapers, wire services and licensed digital outlets across Africa's established, regulated media industry.

Fields

Ownership & reach

Who owns an outlet, how it's licensed, and independently sourced reach figures — the fields CivicSignal's other tools reference back to.

Method

Mapped once, kept current

Built through direct research rather than automated discovery, then revisited on a maintenance cycle as ownership and status change.
