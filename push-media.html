What it monitors

Three channels a web-crawling pipeline can't reach on its own.

CivicSignal's main discovery cascade (RSS → sitemap → homepage) finds what publishers put on the open web. Push Media exists for everything they publish somewhere else instead.

Newsletters

Email-native publishing

Newsletters and email bulletins from outlets ranging from major wires (Bloomberg, Financial Times) to independent African newsrooms — captured at the point of delivery, not scraped after the fact.

Telegram

Public channel monitoring

Public Telegram channels, read via their own public preview pages — no login, no API key. A real and growing distribution channel across West and North Africa in particular.

Fediverse

Mastodon & ActivityPub

Public Mastodon accounts and other ActivityPub-based outlets, read via the same open, unauthenticated protocol the fediverse is built on.

Why this layer matters

The audience has already moved to messaging apps. Monitoring has to follow.

This isn't a hypothetical. It's what the two largest studies of African news consumption and media ecosystems this year both independently found.

Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2026 — Kenya

WhatsApp reach for news jumped to 62% (+16pp) and TikTok to 58% (+20pp) year-on-year. The report calls out Kenya specifically for the prominence of its "creator layer" on messaging and video platforms — not on news websites.

Project Oasis 2025 Report — Platformisation

"News Verifier Africa in Nigeria and Newsghana24 in Ghana use WhatsApp as their primary news platform" — publishers whose main channel a standard website crawl would simply never see.

An honest note on attribution. Some Telegram channels aggregate content from many different publishers rather than belonging to one outlet — a single channel can carry bylines from a dozen unrelated newsrooms. CivicSignal only attributes a channel's own reach (subscriber counts, etc.) back to a specific publisher when that mapping is unambiguous. Everything else is still ingested and classified — it's just not credited to the wrong owner.
